Overview of Data Storage

myfrnd operates without any online accounts, user logins, or remote server databases. All data created in the app (such as to-do notes, reminder intervals, and daily habit logs) is stored 100% locally on your device.

How to Delete Your Data

Because no data is ever transmitted or stored on external cloud servers, you can permanently erase all your data at any time through any of the following methods:

Method 1: Delete Individual Reminders in App

Inside the myfrnd app dashboard, find any scheduled task under "Work Reminders" and tap the Trash (🗑️) icon. The item is deleted immediately and permanently from your device.

Method 2: Wipe All App Data via Android Settings

  1. Open your Android device Settings.
  2. Tap Apps (or Apps & Notifications).
  3. Select myfrnd from the list.
  4. Tap Storage & cache.
  5. Tap Clear Storage (or Clear Data).

This instantly and permanently deletes all preferences, task reminders, and local files stored by the app.

Method 3: Uninstalling the Application

Uninstalling the myfrnd app from your phone automatically and irreversibly removes all local app storage and cached database records.

Method 4: Contact Developer Support

If you have any questions or need confirmation regarding data deletion, please contact us directly at dutypein@gmail.com. Since we do not maintain cloud servers with personal data, we will gladly assist you in verifying complete local device erasure.
