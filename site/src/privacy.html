Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026. Last updated: November 14, 2026 (added section 7 about this website).

Application name: myfrnd (also listed as myfrnd: Daily Companion & Habits)

Package name / application ID: com.myfrnd.app

Developer / organization: vamsib-298

Contact email: dutypein@gmail.com

Privacy commitment: myfrnd is built offline and private by design. We do not collect, sell, monetize, transmit, or store any of your personal information on servers. Everything you enter stays on your device.

1. Information we collect

We practice complete data minimization:

  • No personal identifiers: we do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, biometric data, or location.
  • Locally stored data: the reminders, timer settings, and preferences you set are stored only on your device. This data is never sent to us or to any third party.
  • No internet access: the app does not request Android's Internet permission, so it cannot send your data anywhere.

2. Android permissions and how they are used

To provide an on-screen companion, the app requests these system permissions:

  • Display over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): lets your companion appear briefly over the apps you use, to remind you to rest your eyes, drink water, or do a task you added.
  • Foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE): runs only after you enable the companion, tracking unlocked screen time and showing reminders. Pause stops the service. Reminders wait while the phone is locked and may be delayed by Android; they are not exact alarms or medical advice.
  • Post notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): used on Android 13 and newer to show the companion status notification, with a Pause button so you stay in control.
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): resumes the companion after a restart only if you had enabled it and the permission is still granted. Android may restrict this; reopen the app if needed.

3. Speech, third-party tracking and advertising

Optional speech is off by default. When you turn it on, reminder text is passed to your installed Android speech engine using a voice reported as available offline. No microphone is used. If a matching offline voice is unavailable, the reminder stays text-only. Your speech engine is a separate system component governed by its provider.

The app contains no third-party trackers, advertising SDKs (such as Google AdMob or Meta Audience Network), or analytics frameworks. If the app is distributed through Google Play, Google's own handling is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal information from any user, including children under the age of 13.

5. Data retention and deletion

You can delete reminders one by one in the app, or delete all preferences and reminders with Android Settings > Apps > myfrnd > Storage > Clear storage. Clearing only the cache does not delete saved reminders. See the data deletion page for step-by-step instructions.

6. Contact us

Questions or feedback about this policy: dutypein@gmail.com (developer: vamsib-298).

7. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics, trackers, or third-party scripts; the 3D viewer is served from this site. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.
