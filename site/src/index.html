Your everyday companion

A little care.
A better day.

myfrnd is a free Android app with a friendly 3D companion. He reminds you to rest your eyes, drink water, and remember the things that matter.

  • Android 8.0 or newer
  • No account
  • No ads
  • Works offline
Get it on GitHub See what it does

Not on Google Play yet. You can build the app from its source code on GitHub.

What myfrnd does

Small, kind nudges while you use your phone. You choose how often.

👀

Rest your eyes

Every 15, 20, 30 or 45 minutes, he asks you to look at something about 20 feet away for 20 seconds.

💧

Drink water

A gentle nudge every 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes to take a sip of water.

📝

Your reminders

Add your own reminders. Pick a quick time or choose an exact date and time.

  • In 15 min
  • In 1 hour
  • Next 8 PM
  • Tomorrow 9 AM
📱

He visits your screen

With your permission, he appears over other apps for a few seconds and then leaves on his own. Tap him to close it sooner.

  • Surprise me
  • Drop in
  • Corner
  • Rise up
🔊

Speaks if you want

Turn on "Speak reminders aloud" to hear reminders in an offline voice. It is off by default and never uses the microphone.

🌿

Calm mode

"Reduce motion" keeps your companion still and skips the entrance animations.

A companion that feels alive

  • He breathes and shifts his weight while he waits.
  • He nods while he talks to you.
  • Tap him and he nods and tilts his head.
  • On an eye break, he leans back and looks into the distance.
  • Sparkles, water drops, an eye glow and sound waves match each reminder.
  • Try the mood buttons on the dashboard: Cheer, Water, Eyes, Focus and Drop.

Start in three steps

  1. Open myfrnd

    No sign-up. Your dashboard and companion are ready.

  2. Allow display over apps

    So he can visit you while you use other apps. You can pause anytime.

  3. Turn on your companion

    Tap "Preview on my screen" to meet him right away.

Private by design

  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no trackers.

  • No microphone. Speech is optional and uses your phone's offline voice.
  • Delete one reminder in the app, or everything at once by clearing the app's storage.

Questions

Is myfrnd free?

Yes. There are no ads and nothing to buy.

Where can I get it?

myfrnd is not on Google Play yet. The source code is on GitHub, and the README explains how to build and install it.

Which phones does it work on?

Android 8.0 or newer. On a phone's first launch, the 3D companion can take a moment to appear while the phone prepares its graphics.

Can I use it as an alarm clock?

No. Reminders show while your screen is on and unlocked, and Android may delay them. Please do not use myfrnd for medicine or anything urgent.

Why does it ask to "display over other apps"?

That is how your companion can appear for a few seconds while you use other apps. You can pause him anytime from the app or the notification.

Does it use the internet or my microphone?

No. The app has no internet permission and never uses the microphone.
