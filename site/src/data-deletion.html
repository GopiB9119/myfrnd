Application: myfrnd (com.myfrnd.app)

Developer: vamsib-298

Contact support: dutypein@gmail.com

Where your data is stored

myfrnd has no online accounts, logins, or server databases. Everything you create in the app (your reminders, timer intervals, and settings) is stored only on your device. There is no account to delete.

How to delete your data

Because nothing is sent to a server, you can permanently erase your data at any time in any of these ways:

1. Delete one reminder in the app

Open myfrnd. Under Your reminders, tap Delete next to the reminder, then confirm. It is removed from your device right away.

2. Delete everything with Android Settings

  1. Open your phone's Settings.
  2. Tap Apps (or Apps & notifications).
  3. Choose myfrnd.
  4. Tap Storage & cache.
  5. Tap Clear storage (or Clear data).

This permanently deletes all settings and reminders stored by the app. Clearing only the cache does not delete reminders.

3. Uninstall the app

Uninstalling myfrnd removes all of its data from your phone.

4. Contact us

If you have questions about deleting your data, email dutypein@gmail.com. We do not hold any of your data on servers, but we are happy to help.
