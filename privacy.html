Privacy Commitment: myfrnd is built with an offline, privacy-by-design architecture. We do NOT collect, sell, monetize, transmit, or store any of your personal information on cloud servers. Everything you enter stays strictly on your private device storage.

1. Information We Collect

We practice complete data minimization:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, biometric data, or precise location.
  • Locally Stored Data: Any tasks, custom reminders, screen-time preferences, and hydration timers you configure are stored exclusively on your device. This data is never sent to us or any third party.

2. Android Device Permissions & Usage

To provide an on-screen companion experience, the app requests the following system permissions:

  • Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): Allows your companion character to appear gracefully over active apps to prompt screen-rest breaks and hydration reminders.
  • Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE): Runs only after you enable the companion, tracking unlocked screen time and showing reminders. Pause stops the service. Reminders are deferred while locked and may be delayed by Android; they are not exact alarms or medical advice.
  • Post Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Required by Android 13+ to display the ongoing companion service status notification, giving you full control to pause or stop the service anytime.
  • Run at Startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Attempts to resume only when you previously enabled the companion and permission remains granted. Android may restrict background startup; reopen the app if needed.

3. Third-Party Tracking & Advertising

Optional speech is off by default. When enabled, reminder text is passed to your installed Android speech engine using a voice reported as available offline. No microphone is used. If a matching offline voice is unavailable, the reminder remains text-only. Your selected speech engine is a separate system component governed by its provider.

The application contains zero third-party trackers, advertising SDKs (such as Google AdMob, Meta Audience Network), or analytics frameworks. Distribution is managed through Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Children’s Privacy

Our app does not collect personal information from any user, including children under the age of 13. It is safe for all audiences.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all data is stored locally, you maintain 100% control over your data at all times. Users can delete individual tasks inside the app or completely wipe all data via Android Settings > Apps > myfrnd > Clear Storage.
